# Quadro Words — Android-first full product plan
Product decision 2026-11-27: owner's Quadro Words concept only; Kova/Rush not active implementation scope. Epic #202; implementation @Utkuuzun14. This replaces previous MVP limits. Internal increments are engineering milestones, not reduced public releases. Android first; iOS deferred; existing Quadro web daily/Journey maintained separately. No compulsory login, paywall, ad or intro before play.

## Full product and rules
- Native portrait 7×7 board, three offered pieces of 1–4 letter cells, one-finger drag/drop, ghost preview and accessible tap-select/place. Fixed orientation engine foundation; rotation, joker, gold letters, earned clear/shuffle powers included in full release. No timer/forced energy/engineered losses.
- Legal placement atomically fills empty cells. Scan horizontal/vertical contiguous words min 4 letters involving a new cell; longest valid per direction, deterministic coordinate tie-break, crossing words allowed, union cleared once. No gravity; leftovers stay. Refill used slot immediately. Game over iff no currently offered piece has legal placement under current rules. Free restart. Prototype points 10×length² plus combo bonus, calibrated by tests. Joker resolved explicitly; no double scoring. Endless progression with increasing complexity, no fixed final level.
- Seeded smart generator with bounded search, word-prefix and fit analysis; initial 60/30/10 helpful/neutral/challenging hypothesis only. Avoid forced losses and repeated obvious answer patterns; measure 10k seeds, three bot policies, real user test, dead boards and score distribution.
- TR + EN complete UI, dictionary and separate records. Verified commercial redistribution license, attribution and version, Unicode NFC and locale casing, proper noun/offensive word rules, editorial audit, feedback. No unlicensed scraping.
- Daily shared seed, daily/weekly tasks, XP and levels, achievement badges, cosmetic collection, weekly competition, history, personal records, share/challenge links and optional notifications. No penalizing streaks, gambling, paid score advantages or forced notifications.
- Guest offline full core experience; optional account, secure cloud sync, export/delete, server replay verified leaderboards by language/mode/assisted status. Offline scores personal until verified. No chat. Account/leaderboard backend security, RLS, rate limits, versioned migrations and test/prod separation.
- UI/UX: native gesture preview, animation, sound/haptics switches, reduced motion, accessible contrast/colorblind modes, screenreader, portrait safe areas, 320–480dp/tablet; polished game-over, stats, challenge, settings and shop screens. Direct gameplay launch; one-touch navigation. First move <5 sec and 60fps target on reference hardware. No fake UI placeholders in release.

## Premium and payments
- Free: unlimited endless/daily games, basic tasks, personal and competitive scores, offline play. No forced paywall.
- Premium monthly/yearly: no ads or ad requests, cosmetics, themes/sound/animations, advanced personal analytics, archive, regular ongoing cosmetic releases. One-time ad-free SKU is distinct, not lifetime full Premium. Both paid ad-free entitlements offer same optional once-per-run rescue without ad. Mark any rescue-assisted score separately. No paid ranking advantage.
- Price hypotheses only: TR 59.99 TRY monthly / 399.99 TRY yearly / 249.99 TRY one-time ad-free. Owner approves price and commercial terms; Play localized ProductDetails is source of truth. No hidden recurring terms or misleading discount. Clear period/price/renewal/cancel/restore and customer support. No external billing for in-app digital goods; use Google Play Billing.
- Verify purchase server-side with Play Developer API, package/product/token/ownership, idempotency, PURCHASED only, acknowledge in time, RTDN/PubSub and reconciliation for duplicates/refunds/revoke/expiry/grace/hold. Never grant pending/invalid receipts. Secure token/server credentials, least privilege and audit. Offline signed bounded entitlement cache; uncertain entitlement fails closed for ads, core remains playable. Test purchases only with license testers until owner release approval. No real billing account created by planning.
- Backend architecture and operating cost estimate before committing to paid service. No chargeable services without owner approval.

## Advertising and growth
- AdMob mobile, not AdSense/H5. UMP consent info update/forms/privacy options, canRequestAds, age/data safety policy; test ads/IDs only in builds, production kill switch default off. Premium/ad-free: no ad load; uncertain entitlement no ads.
- Rewarded continue after genuine game over once per run, explicit opt-in, explain rescue, only earned callback grants idempotently. Deterministic rescue opens legal move; no-fill/refusal/error leaves free restart/finish. Never ad on board/start. Interstitial experiment later in public release lifecycle only after approval/retention guardrails: initial proposed max one per three completed games, min 180s, first session and first three games no interstitial; natural results only, kill switch. Banner initially off. No forced ad viewing or incentive to click. Real serving only after privacy, account readiness, tax and owner approval.
- app-ads.txt exact AdMob line at https://playquadro.com.tr/app-ads.txt, developer website in Play listing, store link and readiness verification. Existing web ads.txt separate.
- Metrics: first move, sessions, D1/D7/D30, words, completion/game-over, voluntary rewarded, ARPDAU, eCPM, fill, net ads and IAP/subscription/fees/refunds, conversion/churn. Actual provider reports only, estimated vs settled/date/currency, admin auth/read-only secrets. Privacy-aware tracking, no ad-click telemetry or fake events. One-variable experiments, rollback on retention/policy decline.
- Store ASO TR/EN, original assets and gameplay video, web landing, real test community, non-spam outreach, opt-in notifications, natural review prompts without incentive.
